# Scapegrace

Hand-crafted roguelike. Rust backend, browser client. Levels designed in level builder.

## Architecture Principle

The designer is the brain. The engine is the body.

**Designer decides ALL creative output (via level builder):**
- Map layout (tile grid, rooms, corridors, tile types, colors)
- Boss placement, stats, name, sprite — including WHERE it goes
- Monster templates (name, sprite, stats)
- Weapon/armor design (name, sprite)
- Theme, font, title, description

**Engine handles mechanical operations AND balance math:**
- Combat math (attack rolls, damage, HP, XP, leveling)
- Stat scaling (weapon/armor values, monster HP/ATK from budget)
- Progression systems (XP curves, gear compounding across levels)
- Movement, collision, fog of war
- Victory detection (boss killed) and death (HP <= 0)
- Spawning generic/mechanical things: gold piles, potion drops, unnamed monster spawns from templates

## Critical Rules

1. The engine MUST NOT make creative decisions. No placing bosses, no choosing positions for named entities, no deciding what goes where. That's the designer's job.

2. The engine OWNS balance math. Stat scaling, XP curves, weapon value progression — these are mechanical systems that the engine tunes, not creative choices.

3. The engine CAN place generic/mechanical things: random gold piles, potion drops, unnamed monster spawns from templates. These are mechanical, not creative.

4. Levels are designed in the level builder, not generated by LLMs. LLM-generated content is a legacy fallback.
